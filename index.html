Profile

A PGDM candidate in Insurance and Risk Management, working towards reinsurance catastrophe analytics — pairing the insurance training with the quantitative modelling needed to price and manage natural catastrophe risk.

My approach is to implement rather than summarise. Where a regulation specifies a capital calculation, I build it cell by cell and reconcile the output; where a paper specifies a stochastic process, I simulate it and test it against the analytical solution. That has produced a full OasisLMF catastrophe model run with interpreted OEP and AEP curves, an Excel excess-of-loss financial module over a 1,000-year event set, and a 12-trade SA-CCR netting engine returning exposure at default of $23.5M on a $13.9M add-on.

Alongside this sits eleven months of internship experience, most recently in the second line of defence at a life insurer, where the work ran from cyber gap assessments to CRO-office risk reporting.
